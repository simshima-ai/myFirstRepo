About S-CAD

What Is S-CAD?

S-CAD is a simple browser-based 2D CAD tool. It is designed to be easy to start and easy to continue using, without installation or complicated setup.

It supports basic drafting workflows, geometry editing, and export to common formats such as DXF and SVG. The goal is to provide a lightweight environment for everyday 2D drawing work directly in the browser.
